# 📘 SFCMS User Guide
## Getting Started with the Student Feedback and Complaint Management System

---

## 👨‍🎓 For Students

### 1. Submitting a Complaint
1.  Log in to your **Student Dashboard**.
2.  Click on **"New Complaint"** in the sidebar.
3.  Select a **Category** (Academic, Facilities, Administrative, etc.).
4.  Set the **Priority** (Low, Medium, High).
5.  Provide a clear **Description** of your issue.
6.  Attach any supporting documents or images (Max 5MB).
7.  Click **Submit**.

### 2. Tracking Your Request
- Visit the **"My Complaints"** or **"Tracker"** page.
- You will see a visual timeline showing the status:
    - 🕒 **Pending**: Awaiting initial review.
    - ⚙️ **In-Progress**: Assigned to a department for investigation.
    - ✅ **Resolved**: Issue addressed and closed.
    - ❌ **Rejected**: Issue does not meet criteria (reason will be provided).

### 3. Notifications
- Check the **Notification Bell** at the top right for real-time updates on your tickets and school announcements.

---

## 👨‍🏫 For Teachers & Staff

### 1. Managing Assignments
- Use the **Teacher Portal** to assign lab tasks or respond to academic feedback.
- Monitor student participation and engagement metrics.

### 2. Resolving Grievances
- If a complaint is assigned to you, review the details and evidence.
- Provide a clear resolution message to the student.
- Mark the ticket as **"Resolved"**.

---

## 🏢 For Department Heads (HOD)

### 1. Strategic Oversight
- Use the **Admin Dashboard** to view system-wide analytics.
- Identify "Hotspots" (areas with high complaint volume).
- Review Average Resolution Time (ART) for your department.

### 2. Communication
- Use the **Broadcast** feature to send urgent announcements to all students or specific groups.

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can I remain anonymous?**
A: Sensitive complaints can be submitted via the "Private Feedback" channel where your identity is shielded from mid-level staff.

**Q: What file types can I upload?**
A: We support PDF, JPG, PNG, and DOCX files up to 5MB.

**Q: How long does resolution take?**
A: Urgent issues are typically reviewed within 24 hours. General feedback may take up to 3-5 business days.
